Co-op · up to 4 players

Fight side by side

Up to 4 players. One player hosts and shares the invite link; friends open it, type their name and wait for the host to start. Everyone earns XP. Talk with the 🎤 mic or the chat. Works browser to browser (WebRTC) — some strict networks may block it. Keep the host's page open.
