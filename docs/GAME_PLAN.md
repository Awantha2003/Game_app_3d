# WANNI FRONT — Game Design & Development Plan

> **Sinhala summary (සිංහල කෙටි සාරාංශය)**
> Wanni Front kiyanne 2006–2009 Eelam War IV yuddhaye real operations mata hadapu, kathawak thiyena third-person tactical shooter ekak. Oya sellam karanne **2nd Lieutenant Awan** kiyana *kalpitha* (fictional) officer kenek widiyata. Eyaa thamange squad eka ekka Mavil Aru idan Nandikadal wenakan yanawa, mission win karaddi **rank up** wenawa (2nd Lt → Lt → Captain), squad eka ekka celebration ekak thiyenawa. Real ithihasaya (dates, places, units) saha kalpitha kathawa (characters, dialogue) wenwenama penwanawa. Civilians aarakshaawa game eke wedagathma wadak. Passe phase walin aluth missions (Sampur, Vakarai, Anandapuram, Puthumathalan night rescue), squad commands, loadouts, saha link ekak share karala yaluwo ekka sellam karana co-op multiplayer ekak ekathu wenawa.

---

## 1. Vision

A story-driven, **third-person tactical shooter** for desktop browsers. The player leads a small Sri Lanka Army section through the final phase of the Sri Lankan Civil War (Eelam War IV, 2006–2009). The game should feel tense and professional, and be inspired by classic tactical shooters (stealth, binoculars, alarms, sniping, squad play). Its identity, assets, characters and story are original.

**Pillars**
1. **History you can trust.** Real dates, places and formations come from documented sources. Invented characters, dialogue and encounters are always labelled as fiction.
2. **Tactics over twitch.** Scouting, cover, stealth, flanking, squad support and choosing when to fight.
3. **Every life counts.** Civilians are never targets. Protecting them is a core mechanic. The game acknowledges the suffering of soldiers, LTTE cadres and civilians.
4. **One continuous campaign.** A single squad and a single officer's journey from 2006 to 2009, not disconnected levels.
5. **Runs in a browser.** It must work on ordinary computers without installation.

## 2. History vs. fiction policy

Each briefing and debrief has two clearly separated blocks:

| Block | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **HISTORICAL RECORD** | Dates, places, formations and outcomes | Public sources listed in the in-game *Historical Archive* (Wikipedia articles on Eelam War IV, the user-supplied monograph *The Sri Lankan Civil War (1983–2009)*, the UN Secretary-General's Panel of Experts report (2011), International Crisis Group (2009), Sunday Observer archives) |
| **SQUAD STORY — FICTION** | Captain Awan, his squad, dialogue, specific firefights and objectives | Written for the game |

Rules:
- Real commanders and real persons never appear as playable or speaking characters. They are only named in historical text.
- No invented casualty numbers. Where sources disagree (for example, the Mavil Aru gates being reopened on 8 or 10 August 2006), the game says so.
- Final-phase civilian suffering is described with both documented aspects: LTTE cadres preventing civilians from leaving, *and* the UN Panel of Experts finding that most civilian deaths were caused by government shelling. The game takes no side on accountability; it remembers the victims.
- Fiction never shows atrocities as entertainment, and never portrays Tamil civilians as enemies.

## 3. Characters (all fictional)

| Character | Role | Personality / arc |
|---|---|---|
| **Awan** (player) | Starts as a newly commissioned **2nd Lieutenant**, promoted through the campaign to **Captain** | Quiet and observant. He learns that leadership means bringing everyone home, civilians included |
| **Sgt. Ruwan Jayasekara** | Section sergeant, rifleman | A 15-year veteran. His older brother was at Elephant Pass in 2000. Dry humour, calls Awan "Sir" with a grin. He is the mentor |
| **Cpl. Nimal "Nimo" Fernando** | Support gunner | Jokes under fire, misses his mother's kiribath, and organises every squad celebration |
| **L/Cpl. Kasun Herath** | Recon / marksman | Youngest. Writes letters to his younger sister in Anuradhapura. Wounded at Kilinochchi, he insists on returning for the final mission |
| **Pte. Prakash Rajan** | Medic and Tamil interpreter | From Batticaloa, a Tamil soldier in the army. In Mission 6 he is the voice that calls civilians to safety in their own language. He carries the human heart of the story |
| **"Command"** (call-sign *Lion Actual*) | Radio voice | Gives orders and updates. Never a real named officer |
| **Civilians** (Mission 6: an elderly woman, Mrs. Saraswathi, and her grandson Kavin) | Non-combatants | They show what the war cost ordinary families |

## 4. Campaign — A to Z

### Prologue — *The Road to Wanni* (cinematic, skippable)
1. Black screen. Rain, wind, distant thunder.
2. Text: "Sri Lanka. 1983 – 2009. A war that lasted twenty-six years."
3. A map of the island draws in. The six operation sites appear one by one with their dates.
4. Historical caption: the 2002 ceasefire, its collapse in 2006, and Mavil Aru.
5. Fiction caption: "This is the story of one fictional section and the officer who led it."
6. A 3D fly-over of the Mavil Aru paddy fields. The squad is introduced (name + role cards).
7. The title **WANNI FRONT** is revealed, then the main menu.

### Epilogue — *Remember and Rebuild*
- A timeline from 1983 to 2009 with key dates.
- The human cost, described neutrally: tens of thousands killed on all sides, about 300,000 people in displacement camps, and hundreds of thousands displaced in total.
- After the war: rehabilitation, demining and reconstruction.
- Squad fiction: where each character ended up (Prakash teaching in Batticaloa, Kasun back home, and so on).
- A closing message on remembrance, reconciliation and peace.

## 5. Progression — ranks, XP and celebrations

**XP per mission** is computed deterministically from the mission result. Your **best** result for each mission counts, so replaying a mission can never give duplicate rewards:

| Award | XP |
|---|---|
| Mission completed | 1000 |
| Each objective | 150 |
| No civilian harmed (missions with civilians) | 300 |
| Accuracy ≥ 40 % | 150 |
| Completed without dying on Veteran | 250 |
| Difficulty multiplier | Recruit ×0.8, Soldier ×1, Veteran ×1.3 |

**Ranks** (Sri Lanka Army officer ranks; the player is an officer):

| Rank | Total XP | Usually reached after |
|---|---|---|
| Second Lieutenant | 0 | Start |
| Lieutenant | 2,000 | Mission 2 |
| Captain | 5,000 | Mission 4 ("Captain Awan") |
| Major | 12,000 | Replaying for commendations (post-campaign) |

**Promotion celebration:** after a promotion, a celebration screen shows the new rank insignia, the squad's congratulations, kiribath and tea at the camp, and confetti. After the final mission the tone is solemn: there is no party, only the epilogue.

**Commendations (fictional game awards, not real military decorations):** *Water Bearer* (Mission 1), *Ghost of Baron's Cap* (Mission 2 with no alarm raised), *Steady Hand* (accuracy ≥ 60 %), *Bund Breaker* (Mission 4), *Shield of the Innocent* (Mission 6 with no civilian hurt), *Brothers in Arms* (finish a mission in co-op).

| Audio | Procedural gunshots, ambience | Rain/thunder, radio crackle, menu/mission stingers, footsteps |

## 7. Co-op multiplayer (later milestone)

- **Goal:** 2–4 friends play the campaign together. The host clicks *Invite* and gets a link (`…/?join=ABCD12`) to share on WhatsApp or Messenger. Friends who open it join the host's squad.
- **Approach:** WebRTC peer-to-peer through the free public PeerJS signalling service. The host's browser runs the authoritative simulation (AI, objectives, hits), and guests send their input and position. No secret keys are needed, and single-player keeps working with no account.
- **Limits to be honest about:** some strict networks block WebRTC (a TURN server would then be needed), and with a host-authoritative design, cheating protection is minimal. The feature will be labelled *Unavailable* in the menu until it has been tested between two real devices.

## 9. Testing

- `npm run build`: production build.
- `npm run test:campaign`: a headless browser bot plays all six missions from start to finish and checks that each one can be completed, with no page errors. It needs Playwright with Chromium (`npx playwright install chromium`).
- Manual checklist each milestone: prologue skip, pause/resume, mouse unlock, mission fail/retry, save/continue, profile reset.
